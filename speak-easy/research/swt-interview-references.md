## How the evidence informed original tasks

| Reference signal | Original training added |
| --- | --- |
| Follow-ups related to what a candidate says | Hometown, studies, interests and evidence-based personal examples |
| Confusing the meaning of a question | Ask which meaning was intended, correct an earlier answer and recover after a pause |
| Explaining travel or work preferences | Give a reason, acknowledge a trade-off and describe real limits |
| Practical employer conversation | Retail/cafe, hotel requests, teamwork, availability and useful closing questions |
| Equipment preparation and daily adjustment | Power/time checks, housing/transport and everyday misunderstandings |

The 65 new main questions, answer frames, models, checks and follow-ups are original.
The first eight units and their 40 task IDs remain unchanged. The expanded course has
21 units / 105 main questions / 105 models / 105 fixed follow-ups.
Follow-ups are authored practice prompts, **not** live AI questions tailored to a learner's answer.
Models illustrate structure; learners should replace their facts with their own experiences.

## A realistic practice route

- First pass: one five-question unit on a practice day; write three keywords rather than
  memorize the model. Attempt the follow-up before revealing help.
- Second pass: use natural-speed audio and listen-first mode. Answer from your own facts
  and practise a clarification when needed.
- Third pass: revisit lower self-rated tasks and record a clearer second answer.

At one unit per practice day, the first pass is 21 practice days. Repeating the route
can support roughly six to eight weeks of practice, depending on breaks and repetition.
This is a pacing suggestion, not a measured learning-duration or exam-outcome guarantee.
The course is still not a complete Camp curriculum or a complete general-English program.
